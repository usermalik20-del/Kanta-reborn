YOUR PRIVACY · KANTA

Your songs stay yours.

KANTA is designed for karaoke with music you add yourself. This policy explains what the app and website use, what stays on your device, and the choices you have.

Effective: October 10, 2026. KANTA is in development and preparing for internal testing.

Privacy at a glance

  • No KANTA account needed to play your own local karaoke files.
  • No sale of your app data and no advertising or tracking software in the current app.
  • Your music is not uploaded to KANTA. You decide which folders, exports, backups, and sharing destinations to use.
  • Optional features need your permission. Voice search, the microphone, local-network remote, and iCloud have their own choices and limitations explained below.

Your songs and saved information

When you pick a folder in Apple's Files app, KANTA reads the files it needs to show and play your library. It can save folder access, file names and details, song titles, codes, known artists, and your settings on your device. It may make temporary copies of a selected song for compatible playback. KANTA does not add a music catalog or take ownership of your songs.

Three KANTA-original KAR demonstrations and a generated instrument tone are bundled in the app. On first use, KANTA installs private managed copies of the samples. If you remove one, KANTA remembers that choice locally and deletes only its managed copy; Restore retrieves the original bundled copy offline. Removing a starter does not delete your imported music, SoundFonts, or selected source folders.

Saved Favorites, playlists, singer labels and your song-search list are stored in the app's local data. You can export a list of song titles or save a private Songbook, but those actions do not export the audio files themselves. Removing a library from KANTA does not delete your original folder. Some app recovery data or temporary files may remain until the app or operating system clears them.

Microphone, singing scores and voice search

Microphone scoring and voice effects are optional. When enabled, they use microphone audio on your device to give scores or add sound effects. KANTA does not save those singing recordings or send them to KANTA's servers. Live microphone monitoring starts off and is intended for compatible wired, USB or Bluetooth headset audio equipment.

If you choose Filipino/Taglish Voice Search, you can download a language pack to your device. The download provider receives the usual information associated with a download, such as your network address. KANTA uses a short temporary recording to turn your spoken search into text on the device, then deletes the temporary clip. You can remove the downloaded pack in Settings.

The separate English voice search option uses Apple's speech service and may send speech to Apple for recognition when on-device recognition is not available. The text you approve for a paired remote search travels over your local network. Voice search does not automatically reserve or play songs. These voice features are still subject to real-device testing.

Your private website Songbook

The public Songbook page does not contain your song list. To open your own Songbook, you select an encrypted file and enter the separate code shown in the app. The file is opened in your browser; KANTA does not upload the file, code, or your song searches to its servers. The reader does not put your song list into website cookies or browser storage.

Keep the file and code private. Someone with both could open your Songbook. The code does not automatically expire, and KANTA cannot remotely revoke a copy you shared. A downloaded CSV or printed PDF is no longer encrypted, and your browser or device may keep downloaded files or print history.

Sharing, companion remote and iCloud

With the optional Companion Remote, paired devices on the same Wi-Fi or local network can share song titles, searches, singer labels, the queue and playback controls. Pairing uses a code, but the connection is not encrypted. Only use it on a private network you trust.

If you turn on iCloud Save, KANTA can prepare selected app settings and files for your Apple iCloud account. Apple and any other Files providers manage their own transfers, account records, and backups. Uploads and deletions may take time to finish. Choosing Delete KANTA iCloud Backup removes KANTA's saved cloud copies and saved KANTA settings; it does not delete your original music folders or unrelated Apple/device backups.

When you visit this website

The website is hosted by Vercel. As with other websites, the hosting provider may receive your IP address, browser and request information to deliver and protect the site. The public website (not the iOS or Android apps) offers Google Analytics (G-L0QV01MWR4) and Google AdSense (publisher ca-pub-6290349358884298) only after a visitor accepts optional website technologies. Rejecting prevents both from loading. If accepted, Google may receive IP address, browser/device identifiers, page and interaction events, cookie or similar-technology data and ad-request information for measurement and advertising. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. The private Songbook reader excludes both integrations. Neither native app bundles or runs these website scripts. Website hosting records are different from the files and microphone audio stored on your phone.

If you voluntarily send feedback through Apple's TestFlight service, Apple may share your feedback and related test information with KANTA's developer. Please leave private songs, access codes, and passwords out of feedback.

Optional support diagnostic reports

From KANTA Settings, you may turn on Keep local diagnostic events. This is off by default. While enabled, KANTA keeps at most 200 event codes from the past seven days in backup-excluded, device-protected storage. Events can show app/Host sessions, scoring and microphone status, accepted wired/USB output routes and Soundcard engine selection. They contain no singing audio, song titles, lyrics, library paths, names, PINs, passwords, IP addresses or raw failure messages.

You may deliberately prepare a technical report with the app/build version, iPhone versus iPad, iOS version, audio sample rate/buffer and general DSP/output state. The app does not automatically send this report to KANTA, Apple or another company. Review / Share report lets you choose a destination via the iOS share sheet; the recipient may retain a copy. Turning diagnostics off or choosing Erase removes local history and the prepared report, but cannot erase previously shared copies.

An interrupted session marker does not prove an app crash. Any separate Apple system crash report may contain additional information and requires a separate choice to share it. Learn how to prepare a report.

Your choices and privacy questions

You can turn off scoring or voice search, remove a downloaded language pack, disconnect a Remote, delete local Favorites, and remove library entries in KANTA. You can change microphone, speech, and local-network permissions in iPhone or iPad Settings. You control exported Songbooks and files stored outside KANTA through the service where you saved them.

For setup help or privacy questions, visit our Support page. Public support contact details are being finalized before wider availability. You may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws; nothing here takes those rights away.

We will update this policy if the app or website changes how it handles information. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.

For song ownership, public performances and third-party content, read Legal & Content Use.

Optional on-device vocal processing

The experimental Soundcard applies native Apple or permissively licensed SoundpipeAudioKit pitch correction locally to the microphone input. Processing runs locally and optional monitored audio goes to the connected sound output; KANTA does not record or upload singing audio for this feature. The separate scoring tap remains unprocessed. Read the visual setup guide and the third-party software notices.

Live camera background

In the upcoming stage UI, Camera BGV is optional and off by default. With permission, the front camera provides a live preview behind lyrics. KANTA does not save, upload or back up camera video, and this feature does not capture microphone audio. Stop Camera restores the selected background. Capture stops when the app goes into the background or you leave the stage. System screen mirroring can display the preview.
