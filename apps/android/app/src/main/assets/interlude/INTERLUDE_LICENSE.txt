Standby Interlude (assets/interlude/) — Provenance
==================================================

File
----
standby-loop.mp3 : the bundled standby welcome. A composed 13-second backing
                   track followed (after a short gap) by the KANTA branding
                   voice line. Loops on the empty stage.

Contents and rights
-------------------
1. Backing track — an original composition and sound design by Kanta
   Karaoke, synthesized from scratch (no third-party samples). Bundled under
   its own license, BACKING_TRACK_LICENSE.txt, which reserves the licensor's
   right to use the track inside the Kanta Karaoke app, free or paid.
2. Branding line — a text-to-speech voice line generated by the Kanta owner.
   Rights retained by the owner; no third-party material.

Processing applied
------------------
The backing track was resampled to 44.1 kHz and peak-normalized to -3 dBFS.
The branding line was trimmed of leading/trailing silence, peak-normalized to
-1 dBFS so it reads clearly after the track, and duplicated to stereo. The two
were concatenated (track, 400 ms gap, line) and encoded as 128 kbps MP3.
