KANTA Reborn KaraokeLive GM v3
================================

Karaoke-ready design
- 128 General MIDI programs and four bank-128 channel-10 live drum kits.
- Vocal-style choir, ooh, pad and ambience presets for karaoke backing tracks.
- Built-in SF2 chorus/reverb send metadata is intentionally avoided for KANTA compatibility; ambience is baked into applicable source tones.
- 15 live-style instrument families.
- Three keyboard sample zones and three velocity layers per melodic family.
- 32 kHz PCM, compact enough for mobile playback.
- Smooth attack/release profiles for backing tracks and live melody monitoring.
- Original procedural audio only; no third-party samples.

Important
This bank is designed to sound more lively and responsive than v1, but it is still a compact synthesized bank rather than a recorded concert-library product.

Install
Unzip and select kanta_reborn_karaokelive_gm_v3.sf2 in KANTA Reborn. Test program changes, sustain pedal and channel 10 drums with representative KAR/MIDI tracks.
