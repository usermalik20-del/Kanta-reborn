GETTING STARTED

From your library
to your first chorus.

Start with the version for your device. Android is a debug testing APK; iPhone and iPad require personal signing of the unsigned IPA.

Android downloadiPhone / iPad download

Your first song

  1. Install the appropriate testing build and open KANTA.
  2. Choose an original starter song: Morning Light, Sing Together or Starlight Echo.
  3. Reserve or play it, follow the lyrics and try the volume, tempo and pitch controls.
  4. Add a singer name and reserve another song to try Up Next.

Add your own music

Use the app's file picker for supported MIDI/KAR, MP3/WAV and an SF2 SoundFont. Android also supports folder import and cancellation. Selected originals remain unchanged; the app uses its own private copies. MIDI/KAR needs a compatible SoundFont to produce sound.

Open your private Songbook

  1. Export an encrypted Songbook from KANTA.
  2. Open My Songbook and choose that file.
  3. Enter its separate access code locally in your browser.
  4. Search your titles, then export CSV or print if wanted.

Keep the code separate from the file. Anyone with both can read the titles. No song audio is included.

Microphone and voice output

The recovered iOS app has optional microphone and vocal tools, with physical audio testing still open. On Android, open Microphone & voice settings in Control. Choose Turn on microphone and grant permission to see input level and pitch estimates. Input readings can hear backing music. Song scoring is a separate option described below.

To try experimental voice output, connect wired/USB headphones or an audio interface, choose Wired / USB monitoring and select that output. Start with low equipment volume. Each activation starts Dry at 10% app volume, with an app limit of 35%; this does not control your amplifier. Then choose Room, Hall or Echo if wanted. Input estimates remain separate from effects.

Speaker, Bluetooth and unknown reported outputs are rejected. A detected route change or disconnect turns monitoring off; reconnecting requires another opt-in. Android 16 reports all outputs, while older Android exposes only one and additional unreported outputs cannot be verified. Physical routing, feedback, sound quality and delay still need device testing. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Leaving KANTA, stopping input or losing audio focus stops monitored output. For experimental tuning, choose Turn on pitch correction only after the wired/USB output is verified. Each monitoring activation starts with correction off. Stable voice is nudged toward a nearby musical note; uncertain or stale input remains unshifted. This does not follow the song melody, adds delay and may alter voice tone or create artifacts. Turn it off to return to ordinary monitoring. Leaving monitoring, losing the route or backgrounding turns it off; returning cannot restart it. Dry input estimates and scoring remain unchanged. A microphone is not needed to play the original starters.

For a song estimate, choose Turn on song scoring in the same voice settings, then enable microphone input separately. Scoring does not switch on the microphone or voice output for you. A single identified MIDI melody is required; MP3/WAV and ambiguous references remain unavailable. Adjust Score timing offset only after checking timing on your device. Changing the offset, key or pace clears the current estimate.

The live estimate needs enough stable input. At the natural ending, Last song shows an estimate or explains that input was insufficient. Manually stopping or replacing a song does not create a completion score. Missing singing still counts toward coverage, and backing music can be mistaken for a singer. Results are informal estimates, not an assessment of ability.

Your Android stage

In Scenic, choose a photo or muted background video through Android Files. Originals remain unchanged; the private selection is retained for reuse. You can also turn on front-camera preview after granting camera permission. Nothing is recorded, and leaving KANTA turns the camera off. Camera preview moves between the main and fullscreen stage; TV uses a still background.

Remote control

Experimental Android-to-Android companion control is included. In Control, open Companion control on both devices. On the host, select its private network address and choose Start hosting. On the other Android device, enter that address and the full private 32-character code, then Connect. Share the code only with someone you trust: it grants song search, reservations, cancellation and Play/Pause/Next. The code expires in five minutes, and a session lasts up to ten minutes. Stop or leaving either app disconnects it; returning does not reconnect. Song and singer details are encrypted in transit; music and recordings are not sent. iPhone/iPad pairing is not compatible. Physical two-device testing and independent security review remain open. The recovered iOS remote remains a separate unencrypted private-network feature.

If a song will not play

Confirm the media is actually available, the format is supported, and a compatible SF2 is selected for MIDI/KAR. Check device volume and output routing. Catalog-only rows cannot be queued. Keep a separate copy of valuable originals before testing.

Testing preview

These are recovered development builds, not store releases. Android emulator checks and iPhone/iPad simulator walkthroughs passed. Physical-device audio quality, microphone latency, camera, HDMI and AirPlay acceptance remain open. YouTube streaming, ad filtering, Vocal Fit and harmonies are not included.
