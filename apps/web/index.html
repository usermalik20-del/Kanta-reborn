Know what your version includes.

The two apps have distinct implementations. Android includes private photo/video backgrounds, opt-in front-camera preview and optional microphone input level/pitch estimates. Experimental wired/USB voice monitoring and Dry/Room/Hall/Echo effects are included; physical output and delay remain unverified. Optional MIDI pitch-following scores, experimental pitch correction and encrypted Android-to-Android companion control are included. Device calibration, physical audio and two-device acceptance remain open. The iOS personal build has no iCloud Save. Compare the current features →

Testing preview

These are recovered development builds, not store releases. Android emulator checks and iPhone/iPad simulator walkthroughs passed. Physical-device audio quality, microphone latency, camera, HDMI and AirPlay acceptance remain open. YouTube streaming, ad filtering, Vocal Fit and harmonies are not included.
