Precision Engineering

Features designed for singing, not scrolling.

Everything you need for an elite karaoke party, engineered with native performance across iOS and Android.

🎵

Your own library

Import MIDI, KAR, MP3 and WAV files directly. Only music you own and choose ever enters your private player.

iOS & Android
📜

Timed lyrics

Bold, beautifully centered lyrics that track the music in real time with character-level highlighting on every line.

iOS & Android
👥

Singer queue

Named reservations, reorderable Up Next, and saved sets for your next party. Built-in companion remote for a second phone.

iOS & Android
🎤

Voice & scoring

Optional wired/USB voice monitoring with studio effects and pitch-following estimates for solo practice.

Experimental
📚

Private Songbook

Encrypt your song list and open it in any browser with a separate access code. Zero server uploads or cloud lock-in.

iOS & Android
🎯

On-device pitch training

Pitch estimation runs from a machine-learned model on the phone itself, targeting the song's own key from its MIDI.

Android
🎹

Any SoundFont, any size

Bring your own SF2 bank and hear it through the native TinySoundFont engine. Large banks import without a fixed size ceiling.

Android
🎉

Party & intermission

Eight original stage effects with independent volume, plus your own between-song music that steps aside when the next singer starts.

iOS & Android
📷

Camera & personal visuals

Ten scenic presets, your own photo or video backgrounds, and an optional front-camera stage. Camera is off by default.

iOS & Android
🎛️

Control deck

Tap the KANTA word mark for a hardware-style control screen: master volume dial with search, queue, playback, mic, monitor, key and tempo.

Android
📱

Companion remote

Run the night from a second Android phone: search, reserve, cancel and control playback with an explicitly paired, encrypted session.

Experimental
