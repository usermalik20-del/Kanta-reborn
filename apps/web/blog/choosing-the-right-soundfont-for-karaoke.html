Choosing the Right SoundFont for Karaoke: A Complete Guide

October 28, 2026By KANTA Team15 min read

A MIDI file is just instructions. The SoundFont is the instrument. Choose poorly and your grand piano sounds like a toy keyboard. Choose well and you'll forget you're listening to synthesized music.

What Is a SoundFont (.sf2)?

A SoundFont packages recorded audio samples with metadata telling a synthesizer how to play them.

GM, GS, XG: Compatibility Standards

GM: 128 instruments baseline. GS: Roland extensions. XG: Yamaha professional extensions. GM is sufficient for 95% of karaoke files.

Bank Size vs. Quality

50–150 MB is the sweet spot for karaoke on mobile. KANTA uses on-demand loading so even larger banks work.

Our Top Picks

FluidR3 GM (140 MB) — best all-rounder. GeneralUser GS (30 MB) — best for mobile. SGM-v2.01 (250 MB) — best for pop/rock.

Importing in KANTA

Control → SoundFont → Import SoundFont. Any size, live progress, validation included.
