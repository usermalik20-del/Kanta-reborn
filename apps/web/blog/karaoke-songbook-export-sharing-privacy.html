Private Songbook Export: Sharing Your Catalog Without Sharing Your Music

2026-10-07By KANTA Team9 min read

What .kanta-book Is

A .kanta-book file is KANTA's encrypted container for your song catalog. It doesn't contain audio — it contains metadata: your song list, artists, genres, keys, tempos, custom codes, playlists, notes, and performance history.

Think of it as your library's DNA, not its body. You can export it, back it up, share it with a duet partner, or import it into KANTA on a new device — all without copying a single MP3 or KAR file.

Why Export Your Catalog?

  • Disaster recovery — If your phone breaks or is lost, your catalog survives. You can rebuild your library faster by importing the .kanta-book and re-downloading only the songs you need.
  • Device migration — Moving from an old Android to a new one? Export, transfer one file, import. Your playlists and preferences come with you.
  • Sharing without distributing music — You can share your song list and arrangements with a duet partner without sending them copyrighted audio files.
  • Offline reference — Print or view your catalog without opening the full app.

How to Export

  1. Open KANTA → My Songbook
  2. Tap Export → .kanta-book
  3. Choose what to include: full catalog, current playlist, or selected songs
  4. Optionally add a password for encryption
  5. Save to Downloads, Google Drive, or share directly

The export runs entirely on your device. No data is uploaded to KANTA servers.

CSV Export for Spreadsheets

Sometimes you want your catalog in a spreadsheet — for planning a gig, tracking a repertoire, or just browsing in Excel.

KANTA can export CSV with these columns:

  • Song Title
  • Artist
  • Genre
  • Language (ENG / TAG / MIX)
  • Original Key
  • BPM
  • KANTA Code
  • Format (KAR / MIDI / MP3 / WAV)
  • File Path
  • Last Played
  • Play Count
  • Best Score
  • Notes

Open it in Google Sheets, Excel, or Numbers. Filter by language, sort by BPM for setlist planning, or count your OPM vs English repertoire.

Encryption and Privacy

.kanta-book files use AES-256 encryption. When you set a password:

  • The file is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves storage
  • Without the password, the contents are unreadable — not even KANTA can open it
  • The encryption key is derived from your password and is not stored anywhere
  • If you forget the password, the file is unrecoverable

If you don't set a password, the .kanta-book is still packaged (not plain text) but not encrypted. Use a password when sharing with others or storing in cloud services.

Sharing Your Catalog (Without Sharing Your Music)

This is the most common use case:

  1. You and a duet partner both use KANTA
  2. You export your catalog as a password-protected .kanta-book
  3. You send them the file (Signal, WhatsApp, email, Bluetooth)
  4. They import it into their KANTA
  5. They see your song list, keys, tempos, and notes — but not your audio files
  6. They download or locate the songs themselves, using your catalog as a reference

This respects copyright: you're sharing information about songs, not the songs themselves.

Importing into a New Device

  1. Install KANTA on the new device
  2. Transfer the .kanta-book file (USB, cloud, Bluetooth, email)
  3. Open KANTA → My Songbook → Import
  4. Select the .kanta-book file
  5. Enter the password if it's encrypted
  6. KANTA rebuilds your catalog — songs, playlists, keys, tempos, all restored

Note: Importing restores your catalog metadata. The actual audio files (KAR, MP3, etc.) still need to be present on the new device or re-downloaded.

Backup Best Practices

  • Export quarterly — or after every major addition to your library
  • Store offsite — keep a copy in encrypted cloud storage or on a drive at another location
  • Test your restore — occasionally import your backup on a spare device to verify it works
  • Keep the password safe — use a password manager. If you lose it, the backup is gone.
