SPICE Pitch Estimation: How On-Device Vocal Analysis Works

October 22, 2026By KANTA Team18 min read

When you sing into KANTA and see a real-time pitch curve, that's SPICE running locally on your phone. No audio leaves the device.

Why SPICE?

Autocorrelation fails on octave errors, noise, voiced/unvoiced confusion. SPICE is trained on 10k+ hours of labeled vocals.

Architecture

Self-supervised pre-training + supervised fine-tuning. Conv encoder → BiLSTM → multi-task heads (pitch, confidence, voicing).

Model: spice.tflite

8.7 MB, TensorFlow Lite 2.16.1. Input: 100 frames × 128 mel bins. Outputs: pitch Hz, confidence 0-1, voiced flag.

Audio Pipeline

48 kHz capture → anti-aliasing FIR (65 dB @ 20 kHz) → 16 kHz resample → SPICE inference → median filter + confidence thresholding.

Calibration

calibratedHz = rawHz * 1.0087 - 2.3. Brings median error to < 5 cents.

Honest Confidence Gating

Meter stays neutral until confidence > 0.3. No fake scores before you sing.

Song-Key Targeting

Reads MIDI key signature (0x59). Song in A minor → practice A4. Song in C major → practice C4.

Graceful Fallback

SPICE → autocorrelation → null estimator. Never crashes.

Privacy by Architecture

Zero outbound connections. Model bundled in APK. Only RECORD_AUDIO, CAMERA, INTERNET permissions.
