Building Your Personal Karaoke Library: Organization That Scales

October 25, 2026By KANTA Team10 min read

A karaoke library of 50 songs is easy. A library of 5,000 songs is a different beast entirely. The difference isn't storage — it's findability.

Curate, Don't Hoard

Every file plays correctly. You know what you have. Quality over quantity. Legal clarity.

Folder Structure That Scales

Recommended: A-Z by artist. KANTA-friendly recursive import.

File Naming: [CODE] Artist - Title [Key] [Format].ext

Codes provide unique identifiers, KANTA integration, verbal communication.

3-2-1 Backup Rule

3 copies, 2 media types, 1 off-site. Backup karaoke files, CSV exports, .kanta-book files, SoundFonts.

Private Songbook Sharing

.kanta-book format shares catalog without audio files. Encrypted, local decryption, no uploads.
