Building Your Personal Karaoke Library: Organization That Scales

2026-10-25By KANTA Team13 min read

Folder Structure That Scales

A few dozen songs fit anywhere. A few thousand need a plan. Here's the structure we recommend for a karaoke library that grows to thousands of tracks without becoming unmanageable:

/Karaoke Library/
├── 01_Incoming/              # New downloads, unsorted
├── 02_KAR_MIDI/              # Karaoke MIDI files (primary performance format)
│   ├── Pop/
│   ├── Rock/
│   ├── Ballad/
│   ├── OPM/                  # Original Pilipino Music
│   ├── Christmas/
│   └── ...
├── 03_MP3_Backing/           # Audio backing tracks (MP3/WAV)
│   ├── With Vocals/
│   └── Instrumental/
├── 04_SoundFonts/            # .sf2 banks
├── 05_CDG_Graphics/          # CDG lyric graphics (if you use MP3+CDG)
├── 06_Exports/               # KANTA .kanta-book backups, CSV lists
└── 07_Metadata/              # Spreadsheets, tagging sources
    └── library_catalog.csv

The key rule: one format per top-level folder. Don't mix KAR and MP3 in the same directory — they behave differently in KANTA and need different workflows.

Naming Convention: The KANTA Code System

When you have 2,000 songs, "song.mp3" doesn't cut it. KANTA uses a code system you can embed in filenames and metadata:

[ARTIST_INITIALS]-[3-DIGIT_NUM]-[LANGUAGE]-[KEY]-[BPM]

Example: JOU-001-ENG-E-118.mp3

  • JOU — Artist initials (Journey)
  • 001 — Sequential number within that artist
  • ENG — Language (ENG, TAG, MIX for Taglish)
  • E — Original key (C, D, E, F, G, A, B, plus #/b)
  • 118 — Tempo in BPM

Real examples:

  • SB19-042-TAG-C#-128.kar — SB19, song 42, Tagalog, C# major, 128 BPM
  • ADE-015-ENG-G-76.mid — Adele, song 15, English, G major, 76 BPM
  • GARY-088-TAG-F-92.mid — Gary Valenciano, song 88, Tagalog, F major, 92 BPM

KANTA reads this from both the filename and embedded ID3/metadata tags, so you can search by any component — language, key, tempo, or artist code.

Metadata: Tagging Your Files

Metadata turns a folder of files into a searchable library. KANTA reads standard ID3 tags plus custom fields:

TagID3 FrameExamplePurpose
TitleTIT2"Don't Stop Believin'"Display in song list
ArtistTPE1"Journey"Filter by artist
AlbumTALB"Escape"Group related songs
GenreTCON"OPM / Rock"Browse by genre
YearTDRC"1981"Sort by era
BPMTBPM"118"Set practice tempo
KeyTKEY (custom)"E major"Match to vocal range
LanguageTLAN"eng" / "tgl"Filter ENG / TAG / MIX
KANTA CodeTXXX:KANTA_CODE"JOU-001-ENG-E-118"Unique catalog identifier

Use a tool like Mp3tag (Windows/Mac), Kid3 (Linux), or a Python script with mutagen to batch-tag hundreds of files at once from your CSV catalog.

The 3-2-1 Backup Rule

Karaoke libraries represent years of curation. Losing them is painful. Apply the 3-2-1 rule:

  • 3 copies of your library
  • 2 different media (e.g., your phone's internal storage + an external SSD)
  • 1 offsite (encrypted cloud storage or a drive at a different location)

For KANTA specifically, also export your .kanta-book file regularly. It's an encrypted container of your song catalog, playlists, and settings — smaller than your full audio library but enough to rebuild quickly.

Encrypted .kanta-book Sharing

KANTA's .kanta-book format lets you share your song catalog without sharing your music files:

  • Export your catalog (metadata, playlists, key/tempo preferences) to an encrypted .kanta-book file
  • Share the file with a fellow singer — they import it into their KANTA
  • They see your song list, keys, tempos, and notes but not your actual audio files
  • Encryption means the file can't be read by anyone without the key

This is ideal for duos, bands, or karaoke hosts who want to share setlists without distributing copyrighted audio.

Maintenance: Quarterly Library Review

Every three months, spend 30 minutes:

  1. Delete duplicates and mislabeled tracks
  2. Fix tags on songs that show up as "Unknown Artist"
  3. Move new imports from 01_Incoming to their proper folders
  4. Export a fresh .kanta-book backup
  5. Verify your offsite backup is current

A well-maintained library stays fast, searchable, and reliable for years.
