LEGAL & CONTENT USE

Your music. Your rights. Your responsibility.

KANTA is a karaoke player—not a store or supplier of licensed songs. Here's what that means when you add music, sing with friends, or share a Songbook.

1. You bring the songs

KANTA lets you play compatible karaoke files you choose through your iPhone or iPad. We do not include, sell, or license a third-party song catalog, lyrics collection, or SoundFont library. You keep whatever rights you already have in your own music, files, pictures, and videos.

Included KANTA originals: KANTA includes three short, original karaoke demonstrations—Morning Light, Sing Together, and Starlight Echo—and a small generated instrument tone for these demos. They are first-party samples, not licensed commercial recordings or a third-party song catalog. Other songs and SoundFonts come from users, who remain responsible for applicable rights.

Use content you have the right to use. Buying or downloading a song does not always give permission to copy it, share it, broadcast it, or perform it in public.

2. Playing privately is different from performing publicly

Music played at a business, event, venue, live stream, or public performance may need extra permission or music licenses. Showing lyrics, artwork, or videos publicly can have separate rules. Those requirements depend on the content, how you use it, and where you are.

KANTA cannot check your music licenses or grant permission on behalf of songwriters, artists, publishers, or other rights holders. Please confirm that you have the required permission before using someone else's material.

3. Compatibility does not mean endorsement or permission

KANTA supports certain file types and may recognize formats used by other karaoke programs. That does not mean every file will work, that access to it is authorized, or that KANTA is affiliated with its creator. Names and brands mentioned to explain compatibility belong to their respective owners.

Please respect copyright, the terms of the services you use, and any access restrictions. File support does not give permission to bypass protections or distribute material you do not have rights to share.

4. Your Songbook and shared copies

You can export a list of your songs or make a private website Songbook. The Songbook contains titles and other song details, not the audio files. Even a list of songs can contain information you want to keep private, so share it carefully.

The private Songbook needs both your encrypted file and your access code. Someone who has both can open it. The access code does not expire by itself; downloaded CSV files and printed PDFs are unencrypted. You are responsible for deciding who receives those copies.

5. What to expect from KANTA

KANTA is still being tested. Compatibility, sound quality, microphones, voice recognition, external screens, and optional online services can vary by device, file, connection, and software version. Always keep copies of your original files. KANTA does not promise that every karaoke format or accessory will work.

We aim to describe features accurately and respect the legal rights of users. Nothing in this notice removes consumer rights or other protections that cannot legally be taken away.

KANTA Android: music, files and permissions

The same bring-your-own-library copyright and public-performance rules apply to the Android counterpart. KANTA Android can import user-selected MIDI/KAR, MP3/WAV and SF2 files. Personal photo/video backgrounds and opt-in camera preview are included in the current Android testing download. Compatibility does not supply a license to reproduce, distribute, stream or perform third-party content.

Android stores imported files as app-private copies while leaving the chosen originals unchanged. Android can export either an unencrypted CSV song list or a protected .kanta-book file. A protected file requires its access code to open. No song audio is included, and distributing either format requires permission for the information and contents you share. The Android build uses Android platform and AndroidX software for audio, camera and interface features; commercial Auto-Tune software or third-party commercial song catalogs are not bundled. The original KANTA demonstrations are first-party samples.

The Android app remains a pre-release project. A debug-signed testing APK is available from our Android page; production signing and Google Play distribution remain pending. Android-specific privacy details are in our Privacy Policy.
